Backgammon

Choose to play against the computer or share the board with a friend.

How to play

  • Roll, select a glowing checker, then select a green destination.
  • A point may hold no more than six checkers in this version.
  • Hit a single opposing checker to send it to the bar.
  • Checkers on the bar must re-enter before any other move.
  • Doubles give four moves. Bear off all 15 checkers to win.

The game will open in full screen. Press Esc at any time to leave full screen.

Start a new game?

The current board will be reset. Your player names, board style, and match score will stay the same.
