How to play

You and the computer each start with seven double-six tiles. You start round one; the starter alternates each round.

  • Sign in through RECONNECT-US to play. Your tiles, scores, round, and settings save automatically to your account. Wait for “Saved to your account” before leaving if you want to resume on another device.
  • Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard for the computer. Easy plays casually; Normal balances its hand; Hard considers possible replies and blocking moves. Difficulty is separate from placement guides. You can change either setting without restarting the round.
  • Choose Beginner to see glowing placement guides, or Normal to play without them. You can switch during a round without changing your tiles or scores.
  • Drag a highlighted tile from your hand beside either matching end. Beginner mode shows glowing spaces to help you. Move around the end to choose up, down, or straight, then release to snap into place. You can also select a tile and use the arrow buttons. Drop your first tile anywhere on the felt.
  • Doubles normally sit crossways across the matching end. Select a double to choose Crossways or Lengthways before dragging it. A double continues the same chain; play continues at either exposed end.
  • If you cannot play, draw one tile at a time until you can. Pass only when the draw pile is empty and no tile fits.
  • Empty your hand to win. If both players pass, the lower pip total wins; equal totals draw.
  • The winner scores the total pips left in the opponent’s hand. A draw scores zero. Play as many rounds as you like.
