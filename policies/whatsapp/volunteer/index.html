Volunteer WhatsApp Policy

Purpose

This policy sets out the rules and expectations for using WhatsApp for RECONNECT-US volunteer activities. It is designed to protect service users, volunteers, and the organisation by ensuring confidentiality, safeguarding, and professional communication at all times.

1. Approved Use

  • WhatsApp may only be used for RECONNECT-US-related communication that cannot reasonably be handled through official email, in-person meetings, or other agreed channels.
  • Only RECONNECT-US-approved WhatsApp groups and chats may be used for volunteer coordination.
  • No personal, unrelated, or social conversations should take place in official groups unless clearly agreed for team-building purposes.

2. Confidentiality & Data Protection

  • Do not share any personal data of service users, including names, addresses, phone numbers, licence conditions, or criminal history, in any WhatsApp chat.
  • Any case-related details must be discussed in person, by phone when secure, or through encrypted official systems.
  • Screenshots or forwarding of RECONNECT-US WhatsApp messages to unauthorised individuals is strictly prohibited.

3. Professional Conduct

  • Always communicate respectfully and professionally. Avoid slang, offensive jokes, or any language that could be considered discriminatory, aggressive, or inappropriate.
  • No messages should be sent before 8:00 AM or after 5:00 PM, unless there is an agreed urgent matter.
  • Do not share images or media unless they are directly relevant to RECONNECT-US activities and pre-approved by the relevant manager.

4. Group Management

  • Only the group admin or admins appointed by RECONNECT-US may add or remove members.
  • Group admins have the right to delete any messages that breach this policy.
  • Volunteers must immediately leave any group if they are no longer part of RECONNECT-US activities.

5. Safeguarding & Boundaries

  • Volunteers must not privately message service users through WhatsApp unless specifically authorised for a project, and only through a RECONNECT-US-issued phone or account.
  • Any safeguarding concerns identified in WhatsApp messages must be reported immediately to the safeguarding lead.
  • Never share your personal phone number with service users unless approved by management.

6. Breach of Policy

Failure to follow this policy may result in:

  • A formal warning
  • Removal from WhatsApp groups
  • Suspension or termination of the volunteering role

Acknowledgement

All volunteers must sign to confirm they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy.
