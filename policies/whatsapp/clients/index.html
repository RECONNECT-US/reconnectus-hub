Communication Policy

Client Communication via WhatsApp

Professional standards for staff and volunteers communicating with clients through WhatsApp.

1. Purpose

This policy sets out how staff and volunteers of RECONNECT-US may use WhatsApp when communicating with clients. It aims to ensure clear boundaries, safeguard confidentiality, and maintain professional standards at all times.

2. Acceptable Use

  • WhatsApp may be used only for official RECONNECT-US purposes, such as appointment reminders, check-ins, session links, or agreed communication with clients.
  • Staff must use a RECONNECT-US work phone or number. Personal numbers must not be used for client contact.
  • Group chats with clients are not permitted unless formally approved by management and safeguarded by a second staff member.
  • WhatsApp voice calls and video calls should only be used if pre-arranged and logged.

3. Confidentiality and Safeguarding

  • No sensitive personal information, such as case details, medical records, or addresses, should be shared over WhatsApp.
  • Chats should be kept professional. Do not use slang, emojis, or language that could be misinterpreted.
  • Screenshots of WhatsApp conversations should not be shared, except where there is a safeguarding concern that must be escalated immediately.
  • All safeguarding concerns raised through WhatsApp must be reported in line with the RECONNECT-US Safeguarding Policy.

4. Record Keeping

  • WhatsApp should be treated as a communication tool, not as a case record.
  • Key information from WhatsApp exchanges must be logged in the client’s official record, case notes, or database.
  • Staff must delete WhatsApp chats relating to clients when the case is closed, in line with the RECONNECT-US data retention policy.

5. Boundaries

  • WhatsApp communication should only take place during working hours, unless emergency contact has been agreed in advance.
  • Staff must not friend or add clients to their personal contacts or share personal social media accounts.
  • If a client sends inappropriate content, including offensive, threatening, or explicit material, the message must be reported and escalated to management immediately.

6. Consent

  • Clients must be informed that WhatsApp is used for limited communication only.
  • Written consent or documented verbal consent must be recorded before contacting a client through WhatsApp.
  • Clients must be advised not to use WhatsApp for emergencies and must be given alternative contact methods.

7. Monitoring & Review

  • Managers may audit WhatsApp usage on RECONNECT-US work devices at any time.
  • This policy will be reviewed annually, or sooner if legal, safeguarding, or data protection requirements change.

⚠️ Key Safeguard

WhatsApp is not end-to-end safe for professional case management. It should only ever be used as a short communication tool. All substantive casework should remain within RECONNECT-US systems.
