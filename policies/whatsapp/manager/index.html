Confidential WhatsApp Chats

Rules and standards for managers using WhatsApp for confidential work-related communication.

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules and standards for managers who use WhatsApp for confidential work-related communication. The aim is to protect sensitive information, comply with data protection laws, and maintain professional boundaries at all times.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all RECONNECT-US managers who use WhatsApp, whether on personal or work devices, for any work-related discussion involving staff, volunteers, clients, or partners.

3. Key Principles

Confidentiality First All messages, media, and documents shared in the chat must be treated as strictly confidential.
Need-to-Know Basis Only relevant managers should be in the confidential WhatsApp group or chat.
Professional Conduct Messages must be clear, respectful, and related to work matters only.

4. Rules for Use

1. No Client Identifiable Data

  • Do not share full names, addresses, phone numbers, or any personal identifiers in the chat.
  • Use initials or anonymised references when discussing cases.

2. Data Protection Compliance

  • Follow GDPR requirements at all times.
  • Avoid sending documents with personal data unless absolutely necessary and ensure they are encrypted or password protected before sharing, with the password sent through a separate channel.

3. Access Control

  • Group membership must be approved by the senior manager.
  • Remove any manager immediately if they leave their role or no longer require access.

4. Device Security

  • Phones must be locked with a passcode, fingerprint, or facial recognition.
  • Enable WhatsApp’s two-step verification.
  • Never use shared devices to access confidential chats.

5. Message Retention & Deletion

  • Do not screenshot or forward messages to non-authorised people.
  • Clear chat history from devices when no longer needed for operational purposes.

6. Incident Reporting

  • Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.

5. Breaches of Policy

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of role. Serious breaches involving client data may also lead to legal action.

6. Review

This policy will be reviewed annually, or sooner if legal or operational changes require.
