About this simulator & learning sources

Made for RECONNECT-US digital-skills practice. Android uses a Samsung Galaxy-style pattern; iPhone uses a general iOS-style pattern. These are simplified training screens, not exact copies of a particular phone or software version. Names and paths can differ on a real device. No affiliation with Apple or Google. All accounts, passwords, networks and personal details shown are fictional. When opened through the RECONNECT-US hub, simulator settings and task progress save to your learner account in Firebase. Local file preview keeps progress only while the page is open. Sample details remain fictional; this simulator does not change your real device.

Location pathways informed by Google’s Android location guide and Apple’s Location Services guide. These optional links need an internet connection.

Explore the Apple iPhone User Guide and Samsung location permissions guide. Features vary by model and version.
